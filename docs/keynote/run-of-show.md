## Product narrative

### From assistance to participation

> Suggestions became planning. Planning became implementation and review.
> Now some work can continue asynchronously. The developer still owns intent,
> tradeoffs, validation, and the decision to ship.

### Different execution boundaries

> In the IDE and a normal CLI session, the agent works against a local
> environment you can see. A cloud agent works asynchronously in a hosted
> environment and prepares a pull request. The Copilot App makes the workspace
> and autonomy choice explicit for each session.

### App preview

> The App is a desktop workbench for several streams of agent work. Sessions
> act in a chosen workspace. My Work brings issues, pull requests, checks,
> reviews, and diffs together. Canvases make active work visible when a
> transcript is not enough.

Let the five source videos supply the product detail, with a short framing line
for the purpose of each surface.

### Platform and safety

> Teams can teach Copilot their context, connect it to tools, and repeat
> guarded workflows. The mechanisms differ by surface; the technical session
> and appendix contain the detailed support matrix. More capability makes
> small permissions, visible diffs, tests, checks, and human judgment more
> important, not less.
